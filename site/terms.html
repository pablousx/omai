03What omai does

omai synchronizes supported global AI-tool configuration locally and, when you connect a remote, through Git. It can change native configuration and propagate supported edits or deletions. It provides conflict decisions and local recovery within the documented compatibility boundaries.

omai is not a hosted storage service, AI model provider, credential vault, or package manager for your AI tools. Features and supported formats can change, and running provider sessions may need restarting to pick up configuration changes.

04Your responsibilities

Use omai only with files, accounts, and repositories you are authorized to access. You are responsible for your repository’s visibility, access rules, credentials, and compliance with your providers’ terms and applicable law.

Review what you synchronize. Instructions, hooks, skills, commands, plugins, and MCP declarations can affect what your tools execute. Connect only to repositories controlled by people you trust, keep secrets out of the source, and review conflicts before applying a version.

Maintain independent backups where needed. Local recovery is useful but is not a promise that every edit, deletion, or device failure can be reversed. Do not rely on secret scanning as a complete security review.
